ReelCue Privacy Policy

ReelCue by CDACS · Updated 19 October 2026

On Android, ReelCue uses AccessibilityService to inspect app package names, scroll events, view IDs and visible labels in Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. This detects Reels and Shorts, updates your floating count and returns you to the home screen at your chosen limit. Other apps' window changes are used only to hide the overlay when you leave these apps. Screen text and messages are not stored or transmitted.

On iOS, Screen Time access provides app-usage information used to estimate a reel count and enforce your chosen limits.

Your counts, limits and lock times stay in local app storage. ReelCue has no account, ads or developer-operated server. Android cloud backup is disabled. Device transfer behavior may vary by manufacturer.

On Android, optional Google Analytics is off by default and starts only if you enable Share app usage in Limits. Google receives ReelCue screen visits and feature taps, app and device information, app-instance identifiers and approximate location inferred from IP address. Analytics is used to understand and improve the app. Advertising ID collection, advertising consent and ad personalization are disabled. Viewing counts, limits, Reels/Shorts history, app screen text and messages are never included. Turn the switch off anytime to stop collection and reset local analytics data; this does not delete data already received by Google. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local data. iOS does not include this Analytics integration.

Google privacy: Privacy policy · Analytics data practices.

The optional Buy me a coffee button opens an external website. Your browser and that provider handle any information you choose to share there under their own privacy policies. ReelCue does not receive payment details.

You can revoke access in your device settings. To delete local data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Contact: support@cdacs.in
