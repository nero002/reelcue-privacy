ReelCue Privacy Policy

ReelCue by CDACS · Updated 25 October 2026

On Android, ReelCue uses AccessibilityService to inspect app package names, scroll events, view IDs and visible labels in Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. This detects Reels and Shorts, updates your floating count and returns you to the home screen at your chosen limit. Other apps' window changes are used only to hide the overlay when you leave these apps. Screen text and messages are not stored or transmitted.

Your counts, limits, lock times and daily history stay in local app storage. Friend sync is optional and requires Google sign-in. Google and Firebase process your account identifier, email address and display name for sign-in. Your combined count for today is uploaded only after you turn on sharing, and is readable only by friends you both accept. You can pause sharing, remove friends, sign out, or delete your sync account and friend data. Pausing sharing clears the remote daily total. Per-app counts, settings and past history are not uploaded. Android cloud backup is disabled. Device transfer behavior may vary by manufacturer.

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore provide optional friend sync. Google and Firebase privacy: Google privacy · Firebase privacy.

On Android, optional Google Analytics is off by default and starts only if you enable Share app usage under Help improve ReelCue. Google receives ReelCue screen visits and feature taps, app and device information, app-instance identifiers and approximate location inferred from IP address. Analytics is used to understand and improve the app. Advertising ID collection, advertising consent and ad personalization are disabled. Viewing counts, limits, Reels/Shorts history, app screen text and messages are never included. Turn the switch off anytime to stop collection and reset local analytics data; this does not delete data already received by Google. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local data.

Google privacy: Privacy policy · Analytics data practices.

Android also includes Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity to verify genuine app and device requests to supported backend services. This security integration is separate from optional Analytics. When attestation is requested, Google processes app/device attestation material and issues short-lived App Check tokens. ReelCue does not add viewing history, Accessibility content or banking information to these requests. Security checks do not block local counting or limits. See Firebase data practices.

The optional Buy me a coffee button opens an external website. Your browser and that provider handle any information you choose to share there under their own privacy policies. ReelCue does not receive payment details.

Delete your ReelCue sync account and data

In ReelCue, open Progress, sign in to your sync account, then choose Delete sync account and friend data and confirm. This removes your ReelCue Firebase sign-in account, shared profile and daily total, friend links and associated friend requests. It does not delete your Google account. Local counts and settings remain on your device until you clear the app storage or uninstall.

If you cannot access the app or have uninstalled it, email support@cdacs.in to request deletion with the subject Delete my ReelCue account. Send the request from the Google email address you used to sign in to ReelCue so we can verify account ownership. We may ask for ownership verification before deleting the account. Do not send your password or sign-in codes. You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion. We will confirm when the request has been processed.

You can revoke access in your device settings. To delete local data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Contact: support@cdacs.in
